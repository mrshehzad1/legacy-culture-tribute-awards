THE LEGACY BLACK CARPET EXCLUSIVES

1-HOUR PRE-SHOW

The Legacy Black Carpet Exclusives is the one-hour pre-show experience for the inaugural 2027 Legacy Culture & Music Tribute Awards—the first presentation in an ongoing tribute awards series celebrating the Hip Hop artists, music executives, entrepreneurs, cultural leaders, pioneers, and innovators whose talent, leadership, creativity, and contributions have advanced Hip Hop music and culture.

This is more than a traditional black carpet.

The Legacy Black Carpet Exclusives introduces a new black-carpet experience created specifically around Hip Hop music and the people who have helped build, shape, and expand the culture. The black carpet represents power, sophistication, authenticity, distinction, and legacy—a visual statement that honors the significance of the visionaries being celebrated while creating a setting that feels elevated, contemporary, and unmistakably connected to Hip Hop.

It gives audiences unprecedented access to the artists, executives, entrepreneurs, founders, creators, and cultural leaders arriving for the celebration while creating original programming that sets the stage for the awards presentation.

Live from the black carpet, the pre-show introduces audiences to the purpose, significance, and cultural vision behind the Legacy Culture & Music Tribute Awards while exploring four defining dimensions of Hip Hop:

  • Legacy
  • Business
  • Culture
  • Global Reach

Four Star Anchors. Four perspectives. One black carpet.

THE STAR ANCHORS

The Legacy Black Carpet Exclusives is led by a distinguished line-up of Star Anchors whose careers, accomplishments, and influence span music, television, media, entertainment, business, entrepreneurship, fashion, sports, and culture.

Each Anchor brings a unique perspective to the black carpet. Together, they represent the depth and breadth of a culture that has evolved from a musical movement into a global force.

Their role is to do more than interview the special guests and honorees arriving.

They create the energy of the night, introduce its major personalities, provide context around their accomplishments, and help audiences understand not only who is being celebrated, but why they matter.

Each Anchor owns a distinct editorial lane—creating four (4) complementary conversations that reveal the innovators, ideas, businesses, movements, and global influence behind Hip Hop.

INTRODUCING… DRUM ROLLS, PLEASE

1. Legacy of Hip Hop

KEKE PALMER

Keke Palmer is the host of the #1 video podcast Baby, This Is Keke Palmer and an Emmy Award-winning actress, singer, television host, producer, entrepreneur, and cultural personality whose career has evolved across film, television, music, digital media, and entertainment.

From becoming a breakout young star to building an accomplished career as an actress, television personality, producer, and entrepreneur, Keke has consistently demonstrated an extraordinary ability to connect with audiences across platforms and generations.

Her experience spans major film and television productions, music, comedy, hosting, producing, entrepreneurship, and digital media. Her versatility, distinctive voice, and highly engaging personality make her uniquely positioned to connect with the people and stories at the heart of the Legacy Culture & Music Tribute Awards.

KEKE'S LANE: THE LEGACY OF HIP HOP — Backstage, Keke's conversations focus on the artists, entertainers, athletes, entrepreneurs, and cultural figures whose careers have become part of the story of Hip Hop and music culture.

2. Business of Legacy

TAMRON HALL

Tamron Hall is the creator, host, and executive producer of The Tamron Hall Show, her nationally syndicated, award-winning daytime talk show. A two-time Emmy Award-winning journalist and television personality, Tamron has built a celebrated career spanning more than three decades in broadcast journalism and television.

She made history as the first Black woman to anchor the weekday TODAY Show and has become one of the most respected interviewers in television, engaging some of the most influential names across entertainment, politics, business, and culture.

Her combination of journalistic credibility, national television experience, award-winning storytelling, and exceptional interviewing ability makes her uniquely equipped to take audiences beyond the spotlight and into the stories behind the success.

TAMRON'S LANE: THE BUSINESS OF LEGACY — Tamron's conversations focus on the executives, entrepreneurs, producers, media leaders, record-label executives, investors, and industry pioneers who helped build the business infrastructure behind Hip Hop.

3. Culture of Hip Hop

LA LA ANTHONY

La La Anthony is one of Hollywood's top female actresses, producer, entrepreneur, television personality, and cultural force whose career has established her as one of the most recognizable women in entertainment.

Her acting and production credits include Think Like a Man, Think Like a Man Too, Baggage Claim, The New Edition Story, Power, The Chi, and BMF, while her career has continued to expand across film, television, fashion, beauty, media, and entrepreneurship.

Her experience across entertainment and popular culture has given her a front-row perspective on the evolution of celebrity, fashion, television, film, sports, lifestyle, and media—and on the growing influence of Hip Hop within all of them.

LA LA'S LANE: THE CULTURE OF HIP HOP — La La explores the cultural transformation created by Hip Hop and its influence across entertainment, fashion, sports, television, dance, beauty, language, celebrity, youth culture, and society.

4. Global Reach of Hip Hop

TREVOR NOAH

Trevor Noah is a globally recognized comedian, actor, writer, producer, television host, and international entertainment personality whose career spans television, stand-up comedy, film, writing, and global media.

Following his landmark tenure as host of The Daily Show, Trevor has continued expanding his career through stand-up comedy, acting, producing, writing, and major international projects, connecting with audiences across continents and generations.

Born and raised in South Africa, Trevor's international background and experience working across cultures and entertainment markets have shaped his distinctive worldview and storytelling style.

His ability to connect with audiences across borders gives him a unique perspective on one of Hip Hop's most remarkable transformations: its evolution from a distinctly American cultural movement into a global language of music, creativity, identity, expression.

TREVOR'S LANE: THE GLOBAL REACH OF HIP HOP — Trevor's conversations explore the worldwide reach, cross-cultural influence, international audience, and global impact of Hip Hop and music.
