# THE LEGACY BLACK CARPET EXCLUSIVES

## 1-HOUR PRE-SHOW

The Legacy Black Carpet Exclusives is the one-hour pre-show experience for the inaugural 2027 Legacy Culture &amp; Music Tribute Awards—the first presentation in an ongoing tribute awards series celebrating the Hip Hop artists, music executives, entrepreneurs, cultural leaders, pioneers, and innovators whose talent, leadership, creativity, and contributions have advanced Hip Hop music and culture.

This is more than a traditional black carpet.

The Legacy Black Carpet Exclusives introduces a new black-carpet experience created specifically around Hip Hop music and the people who have helped build, shape, and expand the culture. The black carpet represents power, sophistication, authenticity, distinction, and legacy—a visual statement that honors the significance of the visionaries being celebrated while creating a setting that feels elevated, contemporary, and unmistakably connected to Hip Hop.

It gives audiences unprecedented access to the artists, executives, entrepreneurs, founders, creators, and cultural leaders arriving for the celebration while creating original programming that sets the stage for the awards presentation.

Live from the black carpet, the pre-show introduces audiences to the purpose, significance, and cultural vision behind the Legacy Culture &amp; Music Tribute Awards while exploring four defining dimensions of Hip Hop:

- Legacy
- Business
- Culture
- Global Reach

**Four Star Anchors. Four perspectives. One black carpet.**

## THE STAR ANCHORS

The Legacy Black Carpet Exclusives is led by a distinguished line-up of Star Anchors whose careers, accomplishments, and influence span music, television, media, entertainment, business, entrepreneurship, fashion, sports, and culture.

Each Anchor brings a unique perspective to the black carpet. Together, they represent the depth and breadth of a culture that has evolved from a musical movement into a global force.

Their role is to do more than interview the special guests and honorees arriving.

They create the energy of the night, introduce its major personalities, provide context around their accomplishments, and help audiences understand not only who is being celebrated, but why they matter.

Each Anchor owns a distinct editorial lane—creating four (4) complementary conversations that reveal the innovators, ideas, businesses, movements, and global influence behind Hip Hop.
