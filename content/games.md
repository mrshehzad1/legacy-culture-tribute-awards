### Venue Information

**Gateway Center Arena @ College Park**
2330 Convention Center Concourse
College Park, GA 30337

Gateway Center Arena is a state-of-the-art 5,000-seat sports and entertainment venue located in Metro Atlanta's Southside. It is situated just minutes from downtown Atlanta and approximately a two-minute SkyTrain ride from Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport. As the home venue for the WNBA's Atlanta Dream and the NBA G League's College Park Skyhawks, it hosts professional sports, concerts, and major cultural events.
