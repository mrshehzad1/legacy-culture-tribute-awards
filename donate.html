Help Us Honor Those Who Have Shaped Our Culture.

The Legacy Culture & Music Tribute Awards is a celebration of the people whose talent, passion, creativity, and contributions have left an unforgettable mark on music, culture, entertainment, and our communities. Your support helps us bring these stories, celebrations, and meaningful moments to life.

Become a Legacy Supporter

Whether you are an individual, business, organization, or community partner, your contribution can help support the continued growth and impact of the Legacy Culture & Music Tribute Awards.

Your Support Helps Contribute To:

  • Celebrating and honoring distinguished artists and cultural leaders
  • Creating memorable award and tribute experiences
  • Supporting event production and programming
  • Expanding opportunities to recognize emerging talent
  • Preserving and celebrating cultural history
  • Bringing communities together through music, entertainment, and culture
  • Creating opportunities for future Legacy Culture initiatives

Every Contribution Makes a Difference.

There is no contribution too small or too large when it comes to supporting the Legacy. Your generosity helps us create a platform where accomplishments are recognized, stories are celebrated, and legacies live on.
