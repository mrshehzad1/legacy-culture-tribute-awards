LEGACY PARTNERS

Building Together. Supporting the Culture. Creating Impact.

The Legacy Culture Music Tribute Awards believes meaningful recognition, cultural preservation, community engagement, and opportunities for future generations are strengthened through partnership.

Legacy Partners is a community of businesses, corporations, organizations, professionals, sponsors, volunteers, interns, community leaders, and supporters who share our commitment to:

Honor the Past. Recognize the Present. Inspire the Future.

Whether through financial support, professional services, volunteerism, internships, community engagement, resources, relationships, or strategic collaboration, every contribution helps move the Legacy Culture mission forward.

Become a Legacy Partner

We welcome individuals, businesses, corporations, foundations, nonprofit organizations, professional organizations, and community leaders interested in supporting Legacy Culture.

Partnership Opportunities:

  • Corporate and strategic partnerships
  • Event sponsorships
  • Media and promotional partnerships
  • Community partnerships
  • Professional and in-kind support
  • Philanthropic support
  • Educational and cultural partnerships
  • Internship and professional-development partnerships

Partnership opportunities may be customized based on the goals, resources, and interests of each partner. Together, we can recognize those who shaped the culture while creating opportunities for those who will carry it forward.

Legacy Sponsors

Invest in the Legacy.

Sponsors help provide the resources necessary to produce distinguished events, recognize honorees, create cultural programming, preserve stories, support community initiatives, and expand the reach of Legacy Culture.

Sponsorship Opportunities:

  • Presenting sponsorships
  • Event sponsorships
  • Program sponsorships
  • Community initiative sponsorships
  • Media and promotional sponsorships
  • Hospitality sponsorships
  • Corporate sponsorships
  • In-kind sponsorships

Sponsors may receive visibility, recognition, hospitality, and engagement opportunities based on the level and nature of their sponsorship.

Legacy Event Volunteers

Give Your Time. Support the Experience. Serve the Legacy.

Our events require dedicated individuals who are professional, dependable, respectful, service-minded, and committed to creating exceptional experiences for honorees, artists, executives, guests, partners, and communities.

Volunteer Roles:

  • Guest services and registration
  • Hospitality and honoree assistance
  • Event production and logistics
  • Red-carpet and media support
  • Backstage assistance
  • Administrative support
  • Community engagement

Volunteer Screening & Requirements

Because Legacy Culture events may involve high-profile artists, executives, celebrities, honorees, VIP guests, and industry professionals, safety, privacy, confidentiality, and professionalism are extremely important.

  • Volunteers may be required to successfully complete a background check as a condition of participation.
  • Additional identification, credentialing, confidentiality, or security requirements may apply depending on the assignment and level of access.
  • A background check does not guarantee volunteer placement. Assignments are based on organizational needs, qualifications, availability, event requirements, and final approval.

Legacy Internships

Developing Talent. Creating Opportunity. Inspiring the Next Generation.

The Legacy Culture Music Tribute Awards believes that honoring the past also means investing in the future. Our Legacy Internship Program will provide emerging professionals, students, and aspiring creatives with opportunities to gain practical experience, develop professional skills, build relationships, and learn about careers within music, entertainment, culture, events, media, business, and community engagement.

2027 Legacy Internship Program

Internship opportunities are anticipated to become available beginning September 2027.

Eligibility & Demographics: Designed for individuals 18–35 years of age who are students, emerging professionals, recent graduates, or aspiring creatives.

Potential Internship Areas:

  • Event Production & Management
  • Marketing & Promotions
  • Public Relations & Communications
  • Social & Digital Media
  • Artist & Talent Relations
  • Sponsorship & Partnerships
  • Business Development
  • Cultural Research
  • Community Outreach
  • Creative Content
  • Media & Photography
  • Administration

Internship availability, eligibility, schedules, responsibilities, and requirements will be announced as the 2027 program approaches. Because interns may have access to high-profile events, guests, and confidential information, background screening, identification, confidentiality, and security requirements may apply.

Community Service Support

Serving the Community. Strengthening the Culture.

Legacy extends beyond the stage. The Legacy Culture Music Tribute Awards seeks opportunities to support communities through service, partnerships, resources, education, mentorship, cultural programming, and initiatives that create meaningful impact.

Community Support Initiatives:

  • Youth and mentorship programs
  • Educational initiatives
  • Arts and cultural programs
  • Community outreach
  • Nonprofit partnerships
  • Food and resource drives
  • Creative development
  • Cultural preservation
  • Community service projects
  • Support for emerging artists and creatives

We welcome partnerships with nonprofit organizations, schools, community organizations, cultural institutions, businesses, churches, foundations, and other groups working to strengthen their communities.

The people who came before us created opportunities. Our responsibility is to help create opportunities for those coming behind us.

Why Partner with Legacy Culture?

When you partner with the Legacy Culture Music Tribute Awards, you become part of a larger story—one about music, culture, opportunity, community, and the people who paved the way.

Our partners help create a platform where accomplishments are acknowledged, sacrifices are appreciated, stories are preserved, opportunities are created, and future generations are inspired.

  • Build with us.
  • Support the Culture.
  • Serve the Community.
  • Develop the Next Generation.
